# Amorama

Amorama turns a couple's photos and written story into a short 3D-animated video with characters that look like them. Terms are canonical in English (they become names in code); the PT-BR word the business uses is in parentheses.

## Language

**Order** (Pedido):
One couple's request for one Final Video, from photo upload through payment, approval and delivery. The central unit of work.
_Avoid_: Job, request, project, purchase

**Couple** (Casal):
Exactly two people who appear in the Order's photos and in the Final Video. In v0 there are no pets, children or groups.
_Avoid_: Users, customers, clients

**Story** (História):
The free text the Couple writes about themselves (how they met, a special moment). The raw input to the Script.
_Avoid_: Prompt, description, brief

**Script** (Roteiro):
The structured breakdown of the Story into 4–6 Scenes, each with a visual prompt and a subtitle line. Generated by Claude from the Story.
_Avoid_: Storyboard, plan, prompts

**Character Sheet** (Ficha de Personagem):
The reference image of one person of the Couple rendered in the house style. One per person; the thing the Couple approves before any Scene is generated.
_Avoid_: Avatar, character, reference image

**Approval** (Aprovação):
The Couple's explicit acceptance of both Character Sheets. Scene generation never starts without it.
_Avoid_: Confirmation, sign-off, review

**Regeneration** (Regeração):
A repeat generation of a Character Sheet or a Scene because the previous result was not accepted. Counted against the Order's entitlement.
_Avoid_: Retry, redo, re-render

**Scene** (Cena):
One generated video clip of the Final Video, produced from a Script entry and the approved Character Sheets.
_Avoid_: Clip, shot, segment

**Final Video** (Vídeo Final):
The delivered 9:16 MP4: all Scenes assembled in order with music and subtitles.
_Avoid_: Output, render, result, movie

**House Style** (Estilo da casa):
The single, fixed 3D-animation look every Character Sheet and Scene is rendered in. Not chosen by the Couple in v0.
_Avoid_: Theme, template, Pixar-style, Disney-style

**Order Link** (Link do Pedido):
The secret URL, sent by e-mail, through which the Couple follows the Order, gives Approval and downloads the Final Video. Replaces a login.
_Avoid_: Account, dashboard, magic link

**Concierge Mode** (Modo concierge):
The v0 operating mode in which a human runs the generation pipeline by hand for each Order while the Couple sees only the web flow.
_Avoid_: Manual mode, beta
